Become a Caller

Some of the best medicine is a familiar voice. 📞

The Neighbor Line pairs volunteers with local older adults for one steady phone call a week — a small habit that eases isolation and helps protect memory and mood. This site is run by our school's Generational Club, in partnership with the county Department of Aging.

Proud volunteer partner of the county Department of Aging — Free to join • Dementia-aware training included • Fully student & volunteer run

Why We Call

Isolation is linked to faster cognitive decline in older adults. The Neighbor Line exists to close that gap with something simple: a caller who shows up, same time, every week.

💬

Connection

Real conversation, not just a wellness check — pen pals by phone, matched around shared interests.

🕑

Consistency

Same caller, same day, same hour. Members always know exactly when the phone will ring.

❤️

Care

Every caller is trained in dementia-aware conversation, with a direct line to the Department of Aging if more support is needed.

Three Ways to Stay in Touch

Most pairs start with a phone call. Some grow into more — that's entirely up to the two people on the line.

Weekly Call Circle A 1:1 phone call, 20–30 minutes, at the same time every week. Conversation prompts are offered for the quiet weeks — most pairs never need them for long.
Memory Lane Visits An optional monthly in-person visit for pairs who'd like to meet face to face, arranged through the local senior center.
Family Check-In If a call ever raises a concern — a fall, a missed medication, a sudden change — Department of Aging caseworkers are one call away.

Every match goes through the Department of Aging first.

Referrals come from county caseworkers, so every senior in the program has already been welcomed into other local aging services — and every volunteer passes a background check and a short training built with the department's own staff.

Four Steps to Your First Call

Apply

A 10-minute form about your schedule and interests.

Train

A one-hour orientation on dementia-aware conversation, co-led with the Department of Aging.

Get Matched

Paired with a senior based on shared interests and your weekly availability.

Start Calling

Same time, every week. That's the whole commitment.

One hour a week changes more than you'd think.

No experience needed — just a working phone and a standing hour on your calendar.
